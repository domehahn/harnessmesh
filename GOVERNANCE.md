# Governance

HarnessMesh is maintained as an open-source community project under the Apache 2.0 license.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for project direction, reviewing pull requests, managing releases, and responding to security reports. The current maintainer team is represented by the repository CODEOWNERS file.

## Decisions

Small fixes may be merged after review and passing CI. Changes affecting the peer protocol, persistence format, security model, public MCP tools, or compatibility require design discussion and migration documentation. Maintainers seek consensus; when consensus is unavailable, the maintainers make the final decision and document the trade-off.

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent, high-quality contributions, thoughtful reviews, and reliable stewardship are the primary criteria. Existing maintainers may invite contributors to the team after a sustained contribution history.
